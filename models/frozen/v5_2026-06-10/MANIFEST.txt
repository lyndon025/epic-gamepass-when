# Frozen model snapshot - v5 quantile bundles, trained 2026-06-10

WHY THIS EXISTS
---------------
These are the Phase 5 models exactly as they stood BEFORE the August 2026
data refresh. They are the baseline for the out-of-time holdout test: the
point of that test is to score a model against data it provably never saw,
so these files must never be regenerated. Retraining overwrites models/ and
would make the comparison impossible to reproduce.

Do not retrain into this directory. Do not "refresh" it.

WHAT THEY WERE TRAINED ON
-------------------------
Canonical data as of 2026-06-10, i.e. before the refresh:

  platform      rows   latest arrival in data
  Xbox          2166   2026-01-01
  PSPlus        2661   2026-01-01
  Epic           573   2025-12-31
  HumbleBundle  1174   2025-12-01

So anything that arrived from January 2026 onward is unseen by these models,
which is what makes it usable as a holdout.
