Three problems. The badge is a bucket, not an answer — "more than 12 months" is true of most of the catalogue. The 42% is not the probability of anything, so nothing could prove it wrong. And the range that is honest is buried in a sentence, in relative months that go stale.

We can't narrow this down

Our best guess is somewhere around 2029, but treat that loosely — the honest range here is wider than a decade, which would not tell you anything useful.

▸ Based on the overall average — we have not seen this publisher before, so there is no track record to work from.

Older catalogue titles are the hardest to predict. This one has been out 11 years.

The important variant. Xbox's average honest range is currently 132 months wide — eleven years. Drawing that bar would be technically correct and practically worthless, so the range is suppressed and the uncertainty stated outright. Saying "we don't know" beats a range so wide it cannot be wrong.
