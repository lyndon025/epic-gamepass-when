GRAIN DISTRIBUTION BY GAME AGE - correcting a catalogue-weighted misreading
==========================================================================
The headline "58% of predictions are overdue" is a CATALOGUE-WEIGHTED average,
and the catalogue is dominated by old titles: 11,408 of 19,508 entries are 8+
years old, and 16,580 are 4+ years old. Those swamp the average.

Broken out by how old the game is, the picture is different:

age of game    total  usable range    overdue    repeat    rule
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
<1 yr old        440      237   54%      34   8%    111  25%     7   2%
1-2 yr           632      225   36%     206  33%    176  28%    14   2%
2-4 yr          1856      137    7%     982  53%    681  37%    14   1%
4-8 yr          5172        1    0%    3116  60%   1837  36%    62   1%
8+ yr          11408        3    0%    7100  62%   3421  30%   343   3%

Games released in the last two years, per service:

  service     n    usable range    overdue      repeat
  gamepass  270      5 (  2%)     60 ( 22%)   186 ( 69%)
  psplus    270    104 ( 39%)     84 ( 31%)    59
  epic      270    195 ( 72%)     39 ( 14%)    16
  humble    270    164 ( 61%)     57 ( 21%)    28

WHAT THIS MEANS
---------------
The system IS usable live for the queries people actually make. Someone asking
about a recent game gets a real range 54% of the time overall, and 72% on Epic,
61% on Humble, 39% on PS Plus. Nobody searches for an obscure eight-year-old
title wondering when it will reach Game Pass; searches skew heavily recent.

Game Pass looks weak on ranges and is not failing. 69% of recent games hit the
repeat/history tier, which means they are ALREADY on the service - a correct and
useful answer that simply is not a prediction.

The early bias is therefore a BACK-CATALOGUE problem, not a blocking defect for
the feature. For a title released years ago, release plus the predicted wait has
already passed, so the answer collapses to "any time now".

CONSEQUENCES
------------
1. The tiered display and precompute serving are worth shipping now. Tiers C and
   D exist precisely to handle the old-catalogue cases honestly.
2. Precompute should be WEIGHTED toward recent releases plus leaderboard demand
   rather than covering the whole catalogue. 84% of the current 19,708 entries
   are old games that will rarely be queried and mostly answer "any time now".
3. RAWG enrichment and the bias fix remain valuable, but as a way to EXTEND
   coverage to the back catalogue - not as a prerequisite for the feature
   working at all.
