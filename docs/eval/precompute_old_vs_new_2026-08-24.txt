WHAT THIS SHOWS
---------------
1. Calibration did not reduce how often the model can answer. The usable-range
   count barely moved, 735 to 689. What collapsed was the PRECISION CLAIM:
   month-level answers fell 568 to 101, replaced by year and floor. Same cases,
   honest grain. Game Pass is the extreme case because its conformal offset is
   the largest at 0.80, so its bands widened furthest.

2. OVERDUE IS UNCHANGED AT 58% IN BOTH. Per service it moves by at most one
   percentage point. Band width was never the bottleneck - the P50 centre is,
   and calibration deliberately does not move the centre.

   Every interval change made on 2026-08-24 (conformal, asymmetric variant, the
   refit-after-calibrating fix) was therefore orthogonal to the defect that
   actually limits this feature.

3. repeat, rule and no-interval are byte-identical across runs (6278 / 441 /
   792). Those tiers never call the model, so identical counts confirm the
   comparison isolated the ML tier cleanly.

CONSEQUENCE
-----------
Fixing the early bias is the single blocking defect for the interval display and
for precompute being worth serving. RAWG platform enrichment attacks it directly
by removing ineligible candidates from the censored set (D-019), and the same
enrichment pass delivers the deferred genre, tags and platform-count features.
